06

Silence of the Dawn

Singapore, early morning

Year
2025
Discipline
Documentary film
Tools
Adobe Premiere Pro, Audacity

Watch the full film with sound ↗

Overview

An observational film of Singapore in the quiet before the day begins.

BriefHold attention in a minimal visual context without relying on overt action, while keeping the natural audio clear.

ApproachFilming took place in the early morning, focusing on quiet urban landscapes, subtle motion and ambient sound. The edit prioritised restraint: long takes, minimal cuts and careful sound design.

Process

3 steps

01

Filming

Early morning hours, quiet urban landscapes, subtle motion and ambient sound.

02

Editing

Long takes and minimal cuts, to preserve the authenticity of the place and a contemplative atmosphere.

03

Sound

Natural audio balanced and cleaned in post-production so it stays clear without losing the room.

Reflection

This project refined my sensitivity to pacing, atmosphere and sound as integral parts of visual storytelling.
