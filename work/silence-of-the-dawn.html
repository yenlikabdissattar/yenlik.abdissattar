Overview

An observational film of Singapore in the quiet before the day begins.

Brief

Hold attention in a minimal visual context without relying on overt action, while keeping the natural audio clear.

Approach

Filming took place in the early morning, focusing on quiet urban landscapes, subtle motion and ambient sound. The edit prioritised restraint: long takes, minimal cuts and careful sound design.
